# Portfolio Design

## Overall Concept
A dark, modern portfolio focused on clarity and readability. The design emphasizes the person's identity, work highlights, and contact availability while staying minimal and professional.

## Layout
- Top-level page wrapper centered on the screen.
- Header section at the top with the name and role.
- Projects section below with three equal cards.
- Footer at the bottom with three contact links.

## Visual Style
- Background: near-black or very dark charcoal.
- Accent colour: a single vivid accent such as cyan, violet, or teal.
- Text: off-white for primary text and muted grey for secondary text.
- Cards: slightly lighter dark surfaces with subtle borders and soft shadows.

## Typography
- Use a clean sans-serif stack.
- Large, bold heading for the name.
- Smaller but prominent role line beneath the name.
- Project titles and descriptions sized for easy scanning.

## Spacing
- Generous margins and padding throughout.
- Comfortable line spacing for readability.
- Separate project cards with consistent gaps.
- Footer links spaced evenly and aligned in a clean row or wrap layout on mobile.

## Responsive Behavior
- Mobile-first layout with stacked content and full-width cards on small screens.
- On larger screens, project cards may sit in a row with equal widths.
- Buttons and links must remain easy to tap on a phone.

## Interaction
- Minimal hover states only if they do not require JavaScript.
- Links should be visible and readable with strong accent contrast.
- No decorative complexity that would compromise performance or simplicity.
